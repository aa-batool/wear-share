How WearShare works

The short version of renting, buying, paying, returning and privacy. If anything here isn't clear, ask us.

Renting

Pick your dates on the item's page and place your order. We send the piece by courier to the address you give. It's due back on the last day of your rental, and we arrange for it to be collected.

Buying

A purchase is delivered to you by courier once your order is confirmed. If the piece is booked for a rental or out with a renter, we'll tell you at checkout and you'll need to agree to wait: it can't be delivered until the rental has finished.

Paying

You choose at checkout: cash on delivery (pay the courier when it arrives) or online (a bank transfer or mobile wallet, with the details shown once you place your order). For online payments, please put your order reference in the payment note. We confirm your order once the payment arrives. Card payments aren't available yet.

Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel your order yourself from the Track your order page at any time until it ships. Once it's on its way it can't be cancelled there, so please contact us and we'll help. If you'd already paid, we refund what you paid and email you as soon as the refund has been sent.

Late returns

A rental is due back on its last day. We email you a reminder that day. After that, a late fee applies: one more day's rent for each day late. We email you again while it's late, and the fee is fixed when we receive the piece back. You can see it on your tracking page.

Damage

When a rental comes back we check it. If it's been damaged, we may add a damage charge. You'll see the amount on your tracking page and we'll email you about it.

Your privacy

To deliver your order we ask for your name, email, phone number and delivery address. We use them to deliver, to contact you about your order, and to email you updates about it. Nothing more.

WearShare is anonymous in both directions. The person who listed a piece is never told who rented or bought it, and you are never told who it came from. We handle the delivery in between.
